Mialuna

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Mialuna is a baby sleep sounds and sleep tracking application developed by Kasie Creative Co. We respect the privacy of parents, caregivers and children who use our application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Mialuna allows parents and caregivers to create child profiles and record sleep-related information. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Children's names and profile information
  • Profile photographs
  • Sleep routines and sound preferences
  • Sleep session dates, times and history

This information is stored locally on your device. Mialuna does not currently provide user accounts, cloud backups or automatic syncing between devices.

Kasie Creative Co does not operate a server that receives or stores your child profiles, photographs, routines or sleep history.

2. Advertising

The free version of Mialuna displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from technical signals, ad interactions and diagnostic information, subject to applicable settings and privacy requirements.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and maintain advertising services.

Advertising data processing is governed by Google's applicable policies and privacy practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

3. In-App Purchases

Mialuna offers an optional one-time purchase called Remove Ads Forever.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment processing and associated transaction information. Mialuna may receive purchase status information to determine whether advertising should be removed.

Kasie Creative Co does not directly receive your full payment card details through the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Mialuna is intended to be operated by parents and caregivers to help manage children's sleep routines. It is not designed for children to independently create accounts or interact with online services.

Children's profile information and sleep records remain stored locally on the parent's or caregiver's device and are not uploaded to a Mialuna cloud service.

5. Data Sharing

Mialuna does not currently provide automatic sharing of child profiles or sleep history between users.

Advertising and purchase-related information may be processed by Google as described above. Mialuna does not sell children's profile information or sleep records.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Child profiles and sleep information remain on your device until you delete them through the application's available controls or remove the application's stored data.

Deleting a child profile through Mialuna removes its associated locally stored routines, sleep history and app-managed profile photographs.

Uninstalling Mialuna generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to any device or operating system backup functionality.

7. Security

We aim to minimise unnecessary collection of personal information. Locally stored data is subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Mialuna's features, services or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For questions about Mialuna's privacy practices, please contact Kasie Creative Co through the developer contact information listed on Mialuna's Google Play Store page.
